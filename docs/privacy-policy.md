# Buds privacy policy (draft)

_Draft for review before publishing. Replace the bracketed parts._

**Last updated:** [date]

Buds lets a small group share live locations during a trip. This policy
explains what Buds collects, why, and what you control.

## What we collect

- **Your location, during a trip.** While you are in a trip room and
  sharing is on, your precise location is sent to the other members of that
  room so they can see you on the map. A notification shows whenever Buds is
  sharing, including with the screen off. Live updates pass through our
  realtime service and are not stored; a single "last known position" is
  kept so members who reconnect can see where you were, and it is deleted
  within about 15 minutes of the trip ending or you leaving the room.
- **Trip details you enter:** room names and destinations.
- **Your display name**, shown to members of rooms you join.
- **An anonymous account ID** created on first launch. Buds has no email,
  phone number or password sign-up.
- **Crash reports and app performance data** (for example, which screen
  crashed or how long startup took). These never include your location,
  your name or what you searched for.

## Who sees it

- **Members of your trip room** see your name, your location while you
  share, and the room's destinations. Nobody else does.
- **Service providers** process data only to run the app: Supabase
  (database and realtime), OpenFreeMap (map tiles), OpenStreetMap-based
  routing services (start and end points of a route), Mapbox and Photon
  (place search), Sentry and Expo (crash and performance reports).
- We do not sell your data, show ads or use your data for advertising.

## Your choices

- Pause sharing or watch a trip as a spectator at any time.
- Turn off screen-off sharing in the room's privacy settings.
- Leave a room to stop sharing with it.
- Revoke location access in your phone's settings.
- Delete your data: Settings → "Delete my data" leaves all your rooms and
  deletes your memberships, names, positions and destinations from our
  servers, then wipes Buds from this phone. If you no longer have the app,
  email [contact] and we will delete it for you.

## Children

Buds is not directed at children under 13.

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes.
